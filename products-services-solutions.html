Questions About Getting Started

Will this replace our employees?

The starting question is where your people create value and where repeated coordination consumes their time. We design responsibilities around the work and discuss the effect on roles openly. A project should not assume that replacing people is the goal.

We already use AI. What would be different?

Individual productivity can be useful. I also examine whether shared workflows have improved: how information reaches the right person, how decisions are prepared, and how work progresses across systems. The opportunity depends on the gap that still exists.

Does our data have to be perfect first?

We start by identifying the information one selected workflow needs. Some opportunities require better documentation, access, or data quality before implementation. That preparation can be a defined first stage.

How do we keep control?

The design specifies permitted actions, information access, human approvals, monitoring, and escalation. Initial authority should fit the consequences of a mistake and the evidence available from testing.

We tried automation before. Why try again?

First, we should understand what happened. A technology problem, unclear ownership, an unsuitable workflow, and weak adoption need different responses. A previous attempt can help determine whether further work is justified.

How much will implementation cost?

That depends on the selected workflow, systems, information, responsibilities, and integration needs. We agree on the discovery scope and its fee before beginning; implementation is estimated against defined requirements rather than an untested idea.

What is the difference between the intro, the free assessment, and consulting discovery?

The 15-minute intro is for sharing your priority, discussing fit, and identifying a next step. The free assessment provides an AI-generated Business Strategy Report based on your answers. Paid discovery examines workflows, evidence, baselines, stakeholders, readiness, and practical constraints in more depth.
